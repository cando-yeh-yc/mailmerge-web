客製郵件群發 Mail Merge

貼上一份名單、寫一次模板，用 你自己的 Gmail 或 Outlook 帳號 寄出一封封填好姓名、金額、附件的客製化郵件。 每封信都從你的帳號寄出，會留在你自己的寄件備份裡。

Mail Merge lets you send personalized bulk email from your own Gmail or Microsoft account. Paste a recipient list, write one template with {{placeholders}}, attach per-recipient files, and send — each message goes out from your own mailbox and lands in your own Sent folder.

  • 不經過任何伺服器——名單、模板、附件、登入憑證只存在你瀏覽器的記憶體，關掉分頁就消失。本工具沒有後端、沒有資料庫；流量統計採用無 Cookie 的匿名計數（Cloudflare Web Analytics），不追蹤個人身分。
    No server, no database, no cookies. Your list, template, attachments, and OAuth token live only in your browser's memory and are discarded when you close the tab. Traffic is measured with cookieless, anonymous Cloudflare Web Analytics.
  • 只要最小權限——Google 只要求 gmail.send（僅代為寄信）與 email（顯示登入帳號）；Microsoft 只要求 Mail.Send。不要求讀取你的信件、通訊錄或雲端硬碟。
    We request only gmail.send (send-only) and email from Google, and Mail.Send from Microsoft. We never request read access to your mailbox, contacts, or files.
  • 你隨時可以收回授權——到 Google 或 Microsoft 帳號設定移除本應用程式即可。
    You can revoke access at any time from your Google or Microsoft account settings.
